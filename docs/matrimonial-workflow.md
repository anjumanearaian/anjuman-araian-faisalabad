# Matrimonial Matching & Consent Workflow

## Purpose
The service provides privacy-controlled profile matching and introductions. Compatibility percentages are decision-support signals based on information and preferences supplied by the parties. They are not a guarantee of suitability or marriage success.

## Client self-service
1. An approved client views anonymized compatible profiles and match percentages.
2. The client sends a private interest request.
3. The request is forwarded directly to the other verified portal account. Admin receives an audit/email for oversight, not as a routine blocking step.
4. The other side reviews safe profile basics and the compatibility score, then accepts or declines.
5. After acceptance, only details permitted by both profiles' privacy settings are released.

## Office-assisted / offline
1. An authorized matrimonial manager selects an approved candidate and reviews compatibility.
2. The manager may create an assisted interest.
3. If the target has a verified portal account, the target responds in the portal.
4. If the target has no portal account, the request appears in the Offline Consent Queue.
5. Staff must record the consent method and a short audit note before acceptance or decline.

## Responsibility and verification
Candidates and families remain responsible for independently verifying material facts including identity, age, marital status, family background, education, employment/business, residence/visa status, financial claims and any other information relevant to their decision. Meetings, travel, gifts, loans, payments and marriage arrangements are decisions of the parties. The service does not treat a match percentage or acceptance of an introduction as acceptance of a marriage proposal.

## Printed annexures
The confidential candidate print form includes:
- Annexure A: Candidate / Guardian Information & Consent Declaration
- Annexure B: Matching, Introduction, Privacy & Responsibility Acknowledgement
- Annexure C: Verification & Introduction Record

Final legal wording for any binding waiver or indemnity should be reviewed by a Pakistan-qualified legal adviser.
